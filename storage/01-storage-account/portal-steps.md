# Azure Portal Steps

## Step 1

Sign in to the Azure Portal.

---

## Step 2

Create a new Resource Group.

Information used:

- Subscription
- Resource Group Name
- Region

---

## Step 3

Navigate to:

Storage Accounts

Select:

Create

---

## Step 4

Configure the Storage Account.

Example settings:

- Performance: Standard
- Redundancy: LRS
- Primary Service: Azure Blob Storage

---

## Step 5

Review all configuration settings.

---

## Step 6

Click:

Review + Create

---

## Step 7

Deploy the Storage Account.

---

## Step 8

Open the Storage Account.

Verify:

- Overview
- Endpoints
- Resource ID
- Region
- Subscription

---

## Result

Azure Storage Account successfully deployed.
